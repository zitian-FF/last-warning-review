About this edition

A local, multilingual Last War reference built from original game artwork and verified extracted configuration.

Data and languages

The primary configuration snapshot is39622. English and Traditional Chinese use locale23582; other identified languages use23555 where compatible. Missing or incompatible translations show an English fallback indicator.

Using the guide

Choose a season and language, browse a category, or search across names, aliases and skills. Known later-season content is hidden by default. Links keep your language and season and can open a specific article tab.

Scope

Base costs, stats, skills, research, units, gear, drone, Overlord, seasons and mode rules are included where verified. Internet-assisted values are labelled separately. Account bonuses, live server selection and event schedules may differ. Unproved records are held rather than filled with guesses. Festive events are excluded.

This working edition runs locally; it has not been publicly deployed.
